      Filter

      Free text, "quoted phrases", field=value, or field!=value. Numeric comparisons use > >= < <=; severity names also support ordering, such as severity<=warning. Timestamps support equality and wildcard matching, not ordering comparisons. Wildcards: * ?. Terms use implicit AND; combine with AND OR NOT and parentheses. An empty query includes every event.
